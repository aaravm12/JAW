# Original synthetic fixtures

The generated fixtures described below are synthetic and excluded from git. The generator and timing configuration are committed; pytest regenerates missing fixtures automatically. No identifiable human photographs or videos are included. Fixture content is dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `synthetic_landmarks.csv`: 660 frames of mathematically generated selected landmark coordinates at 30 Hz, 640 × 480 coordinate convention. Four-second resting lead-in, four sinusoidal opening cycles, then rest. Generated by `jrm.synthetic`. This bypasses ML and tests geometry/analysis independently.
- `cartoon_assessment.mp4`: 22.4 seconds of a programmatically drawn cartoon face, 480 × 640, 30 Hz. Two seconds calibration, two seconds baseline, eight seconds controlled movement, eight seconds activity, two seconds post-activity, then a short tail. The real Face Landmarker processes these pixels. Mouth drawing is not anatomical ground truth; never infer human accuracy from it.
- `no_face_vfr.mkv`: 45 original blank frames with alternating frame intervals, for missing-face and variable-frame-rate regression.
- `short_assessment.json`: phase timings matching the cartoon clip.

Regenerate: `.venv/bin/python scripts/make_fixtures.py`.

Analyze the cartoon:

```sh
.venv/bin/python -m jrm --config data/short_assessment.json --analyze-video data/cartoon_assessment.mp4 --synthetic-video --export cartoon.csv
```

In the desktop UI set durations to **2 / 8 / 8 / 2**, enable **Imported video is synthetic**, then Analyze video. Source type `synthetic_video` remains separate from human video, webcam, and generated-landmark demo sessions.
